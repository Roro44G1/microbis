Reviews, 1997–2012

Hurem, a dancing premiere

“The performance ‘HUREM – The Human Condition’ has the cycle of human life as its common thread, each stage we pass through being clearly transposed into the choreographic discourse. With relatively simple props, but with choreography built on extremely varied and coherent movement, choreographer Hugo Wolff manages to bring out every age of human life, leaving no room for misinterpretation. The stories are told through dance, music, poetry and, not least, through the unconventional space chosen for the premiere.”

Tribuna, 19 September 2012

See the performance

The Last Bite of… Love

“Starting from historical scenes, Hugo Wolff, who created the choreography for this performance, has transposed into dance moments from the dark history of Europe. The MICROBIS Dance Company presents a creation set in a ‘death camp’, a story of love and hate told through dance and music. The MICROBIS Dance Company supports and promotes mainly contemporary dance, with the aim of initiating high-impact cultural projects. Although made up mostly of students, the Microbis Company has managed over the years to impress with its original choreographic productions.”

Adevărul de seară, 4 October 2011

See the performance

Adam and Eve

“The dance performance ‘Adam and Eve’ brings passion, jealousy and emotional turmoil to the stage. This is the triangle that shapes the fate of the six couples who tell their stories in contemporary dance rhythms, their love stories scarred by domestic violence. […] ‘Don’t let love die’ — this is the message the MICROBIS dancers want to convey.”

Alina Suciu, 24 ORE MUREȘENE, 26 November 2005

The Court of Miracles

“The 10 members of the group, together with Hugo, succeeded in staging an impossible love story from the 19th century, when differences in social rank were an insurmountable obstacle for two lovers. This story of a ‘he’ and a ‘she’ is transferred to the 20th century, when social rank no longer matters, but when a greater danger threatens a relationship: drugs — the young people’s dance being a protest against this scourge.”

(A.C.) Monitorul de Brăila, 4–5 June 2005

See the performance

A performance about the lucidity of madmen

“The appearance of the madmen in Moments of Awareness brings to mind the madmen of The Seventh Commandment, by Dario Fo — characters who have moments of lucidity in a life where people struggle to survive, and who are often the most lucid of all. The artistic interpretation was interesting: a performance with well-crafted stage design and rich stage movement. […] Their inner turmoil, transformed into movement and grace, every feeling translated into undulations and steps and conveyed directly to the audience, creates a communion between character and performer.”

Liviu Pietraru, Rondul de Sibiu, 22 April 1997

Impressions and appreciation

On Hugo Wolff

“I have seen several performances by the dance company directed by choreographer Hugo Wolff. He is a young artist who works very seriously and is a truly remarkable person. In his art he combines style with delicacy and energy, reviving the traditional roots of Romanian dance in a modern way. His personal talents and his conduct inspire confidence and create an atmosphere that empowers the dancers to reach a high level of performance. Tradition and modernity are the two resources of Hugo Wolff’s artistic vision.”

Eugene van Itterbeek, Leuven, September 1995

“We are all born with a special gene. We are all seekers, and we learn all our lives. Dance has no limits… We learn all the time. An explorer, a restless seeker of bodily expression, with a love and passion hard to equal. This is Hugo Wolff — a consummate man and friend, born to explore and to pass on the art of dance in all its forms.”

Mihai Mîndruțiu, ballet master, November 2011

Press kit

Key facts, for quick reference

  • Full name: MICROBIS Theatre Dance Company
  • Institution: University Cultural Center of Sibiu
  • Founded: 1993, by choreographer Hugo Wolff — the first modern and contemporary dance group in Sibiu, which later became the MICROBIS Theatre Dance Company
  • Artistic genre: theatre dance, contemporary dance, experimental performance
  • Cast: young people passionate about dance and theatre, renewed periodically
  • Awards and milestones: debut with two gold medals (Cluj-Napoca, 1994); recognition at the Leskovac Carnival, Serbia (2010); diploma for Best Stage Design at the “In the World of Thalia” Student Theatre Gala, 7th edition, Ploiești (2011)
