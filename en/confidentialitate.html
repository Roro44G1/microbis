Privacy & data processing

Last updated: [ date to be added on publication ]. This text is a standard template, adapted for a presentation website with a contact form and an audition form — we recommend that it be reviewed by the Data Protection Officer (DPO) of the University Cultural Center of Sibiu before publication.

1. Data controller

Personal data collected through this website are processed by the University Cultural Center of Sibiu, a public cultural institution headquartered at 34 Calea Dumbrăvii, Sibiu, Romania, acting as data controller for the activity of the MICROBIS Theatre Dance Company.

2. What data we collect and why

Through the forms on this website (contact and auditions) we collect: name, e-mail address, phone number (optional) and any information you choose to include in your message or in the description of your previous experience. These data are used exclusively to respond to your request — audition scheduling, seat reservations, collaborations, press enquiries.

3. Legal basis

Processing is based on your explicit consent, given by ticking the consent box in the form, in accordance with Article 6(1)(a) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

4. Storage period

Data are kept only for as long as necessary to handle your request (as a rule, until the end of the audition season or of the correspondence), after which they are deleted or anonymised.

5. Photographs and video materials of members

Photographs, names and video materials of the dancers and the technical team are published on this website only with the explicit written consent of each individual — obtained separately by the institution before publication.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and data portability, as well as the right to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before its withdrawal. To exercise these rights, you can write to us at contact@microbis.ro.

7. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Romanian National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP) if you consider that the processing of your data infringes the legislation in force.

8. Cookies

In its current form, this website does not use tracking or analytics cookies. If traffic analysis tools (e.g. Google Analytics) or other non-essential cookies are added later, this section must be updated and the website must display a cookie consent banner, in accordance with the applicable legislation.
