About the performance

Told through dance, the story of Bonnie and Clyde unfolds the web of relationships of the 1930s, the era of public enemies and of outlaws whose crimes made history. Love and robberies: a fascinating, moving story that has captivated the whole world.

“Bonnie and Clyde” is a performance built around love — a youthful, passionate love, full of adventure and eroticism. Choreographer Hugo Wolff brings out a violent, at times degrading universe that nonetheless explodes into a rare tenderness: the sensitive image of the deep layer born from the pain of childhood trauma.

The story, the choreographic discourse, the props and the staging firmly reject the idea of immaculate white and absolute black, convincing the audience that innocence is not what it seems: it can be lost in a moment of terrible pain and regained just as quickly, with the help of the supreme feeling — Love.
