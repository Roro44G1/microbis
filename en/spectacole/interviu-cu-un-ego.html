About the performance

“Interview with an Ego” is a theatre dance performance by Hugo Wolff, with Diana Cătănescu, on a text by Ecaterina Oproiu. The poster announces the performance of 20 September 2013 at 5 p.m., in the Blue Hall (Sala Albastră) of the University Cultural Center of Sibiu.
