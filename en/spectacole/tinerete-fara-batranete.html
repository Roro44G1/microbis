About the performance

Conceived as a synthesis of thirty years of creation, the performance explores the dance of death and the cycle of human life through a “doubled” character — one side of whose costume bears a human skeleton. The local press described the creative process as one of rare intensity, with Hugo Wolff himself being, at the time, “in a veritable creative trance”.

Context

“Youth Without Age” was conceived to mark three decades since the company was founded. Shortly after this anniversary, Hugo Wolff died suddenly, on the night of 27 to 28 August 2023 — and the performance has remained, in retrospect, his artistic testament.
