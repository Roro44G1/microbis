Ritual and
Red Apples

or what might have been a Greek tragedy

Original title: Ritual și Mere Roșii

Choreography
Alex Wolff
Genre
Contemporary theatre dance
Premiere
9 October 2025, “Ion Besoiu” Cultural Centre

About the performance

You do not sin because you wanted to. You sin because you were told that you should. Because that is what you saw others do. Because in a society that confuses conformity with virtue, refusal becomes the ultimate sin.

Society does not push you; it simply offers you an apple, with the gentle smile of those who have already taken a bite, and whispers: it’s time. Everyone has tasted it. Everyone says it’s natural. What is the point of resisting, when resistance means isolation?

On stage, bodies speak about what we are afraid to admit: the fear of being different, the need to be loved at any cost, the exhaustion of pretending you are fine, the shame of feeling.

The performance is a collective confession, a circle in which guilt becomes dance, and dance becomes a form of survival. A ritual about how we end up living other people’s lives, hiding our own desires in the pockets of shame.

It is a story about how sin becomes tradition. About how, over time, mistakes are no longer condemned — they are repeated, learned, inherited. And in the end, when the taste of the apple clings to the roof of your mouth and you no longer know whether it is sweet or bitter, one unavoidable question remains:

Was it your choice? Or merely one step in an old dance, repeated endlessly, with your eyes closed?

Context

“Ritual and Red Apples” opened the second edition of the “Hugo Wolff” Dance Festival before a full house, on 9 October 2025, at the “Ion Besoiu” Cultural Centre in Sibiu — a festival organised by the University Cultural Center of Sibiu in memory of the company’s founder. The local press described the evening as “a memorable opening”, a performance that “impressed with its intensity, constant energy and powerful symbols”.
