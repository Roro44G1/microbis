The Collector

About the performance

The performance tells the story of a man who ages physically but remains psychologically powerful — and from this comes his power to manipulate the world, especially women. His supreme pleasure is to “collect” women of different races, shapes, ages, types and personalities, whom he uses in his own Machiavellian way. At some point he drives them to suicide or even kills them, to vary his pleasures. He reaches the heights of human perversity, in defiance of the dogmas imposed by society.

But one day he meets someone who unsettles him. She withstands every challenge because she has built several personalities that she can abandon or return to at will. She can also switch between them instinctively whenever he attacks her. Trying to control her, he falls into his own trap and becomes dependent on her love.

He himself ends up ready to give up everything, even his life, just to be noticed and loved by her. His obsession with becoming whole turns manic. After a spiritually and physically perfect relationship, leading to ancestral, almost fantastical discoveries, an utterly trivial event makes her realise his true intentions — and when she understands that she cannot bring him back to reality, she decides that he must die, not her. How will all of this come about?
