About the performance

A theatre dance performance. Producer: University Cultural Center of Sibiu, MICROBIS Theatre Dance Company. Poster design: Mihai Panaitescu.

From the press

“Starting from historical scenes, Hugo Wolff, who created the choreography for this performance, has transposed into dance moments from the dark history of Europe. The MICROBIS Dance Company presents a creation set in a ‘death camp’, a story of love and hate told through dance and music. The MICROBIS Dance Company supports and promotes mainly contemporary dance, with the aim of initiating high-impact cultural projects. Although made up mostly of students, the Microbis Company has managed over the years to impress with its original choreographic productions.”

Adevărul de seară, 4 October 2011
