About the performance

Moments of Awareness is an intimate and profound performance that reveals the inner universe of 13 people brought together in a sanatorium, each with their own suffering hidden behind a mask. In this isolated place, where time seems suspended, each character experiences moments of lucidity in which their past and traumas come to the surface.

Throughout the performance, we glimpse fragments of life: characters with stories that have marked their existence and that unfold like an emotional puzzle, revealing the personal drama that brought them to this place of pain.

As they coexist in this place of suffering, the characters discover that, although they hide their wounds, they are bound by a common desire for healing and understanding. In these moments of lucidity, barriers break, “masks fall”, and the performance takes us on a journey through trauma and human fragility, to a place where no one would want to go, but which, paradoxically, reveals universal truths about loss, acceptance and hope.

The performance explores human vulnerability and the complexity of relationships, in a symbolic setting that reflects the tension between suffering and the desire for liberation.

Context

Moments of Awareness was first created in 1993 by Hugo Wolff as one of the early contemporary dance works of the MICROBIS Theatre Dance Company. In its present reimagining, under the direction of Florian Oros, the original themes and choreography remain faithful to the original creation, while new layers of psychological depth emerge. Through deeply personal contributions from the dancers, the performance gains renewed intensity, becoming a living dialogue between past and present.

The performance was presented on 27 November 2024 at the University Cultural Center of Alba Iulia, as part of the 28th edition of the “Serbările Unirii” National Student Festival.
