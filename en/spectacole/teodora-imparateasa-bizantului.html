About the performance

A theatre dance performance created through the “Artists in Residence” programme. Organisers: the University Cultural Center of Sibiu and the Conexiuni Association. Funders: the Ministry of Education, Research and Youth, the National Authority for Youth, the Local Council through Sibiu City Hall, and the Sibiu Municipal House of Culture.
