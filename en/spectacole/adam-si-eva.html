Adam and Eve

Original title: Adam și Eva

Genre
Theatre dance
Producer
University Cultural Center of Sibiu
Premiere
2005, at the Dance Festival

From the press

“The dance performance ‘Adam and Eve’ brings passion, jealousy and emotional turmoil to the stage. This is the triangle that shapes the fate of the six couples who tell their stories in contemporary dance rhythms, their love stories scarred by domestic violence. This is one of the serious problems society faces, which is also why ‘Adam and Eve’ is a protest and a struggle of young people against domestic violence. ‘Don’t let love die’ — this is the message the MICROBIS dancers want to convey. The performance is the third production made entirely by the University Cultural Center of Sibiu.”

Alina Suciu, 24 ORE MUREȘENE, 26 November 2005
