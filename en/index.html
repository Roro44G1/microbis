Microbis is more than a Theatre Dance Company — it is thirty years of generations of young people passionate about dance and theatre who have followed one another on the same stage, guided by the same idea: that movement can say what words cannot.

Identity

Theatre, dance and live music in a single language

Microbis is the Theatre Dance Company of the University Cultural Center of Sibiu — an artistic formula that combines contemporary dance technique with theatricality and with music performed live on stage rather than played from recordings. The company’s performances do not illustrate stories; they build worlds: rituals, states, cycles — guilt, lucidity, liberation, the passing of time.

What has kept the company alive for thirty years: its cast is made up of young people passionate about dance and theatre. Each generation that passes through Microbis brings new energy, yet inherits the same discipline and the same search for authenticity handed down since its founding.

History

Thirty-three years in brief

1984

Hugo Wolff begins dancing at the Popular School of Arts in Sibiu, under the guidance of his teacher, Ibolya Cristea.

1993

Wolff founds Microbis — the first modern and contemporary dance group in Sibiu, which later became the MICROBIS Theatre Dance Company. “In 1993 there was no modern and contemporary dance company or group in Sibiu,” he would later recall.

1994

The company’s debut: two gold medals at a dance festival and competition held in Cluj-Napoca.

2010

International recognition at the Leskovac Carnival, Serbia.

2011

Diploma for Best Stage Design at the “In the World of Thalia” (“În lumea Thaliei”) Student Theatre Gala, 7th edition, Ploiești.

2023

The company marks 30 years of activity with its testament performance “Youth Without Age” (Tinerețe fără bătrânețe). Shortly afterwards, Hugo Wolff dies suddenly — his last choreography for Microbis remains, in retrospect, his own artistic testament.

2024 — 2025

A new generation takes over the company. The University Cultural Center of Sibiu launches the “Hugo Wolff” Dance Festival, with Microbis as host company. The company presents “Moments of Awareness” (Clipe de luciditate) at the “Serbările Unirii” National Student Festival in Alba Iulia, and premieres “Ritual and Red Apples” (Ritual și Mere Roșii) at the second edition of the festival named after Hugo Wolff.

The founder

Hugo Wolff — “the father of modern dance in Sibiu”

Choreographer and founder of the Microbis company, Hugo Wolff led it without interruption for three decades, creating its entire repertoire throughout that time. In parallel, he pursued his own career as a choreographer, working for more than twenty years with the “Tony Bulandra” Theatre in Târgoviște — Hamlet, Romeo and Juliet, Carmen, The Cherry Orchard — and choreographed street performances for the Sibiu International Theatre Festival between 2003 and 2008. In 2012 he received the title of “Best Choreographer” in Turkey for Hamlet.

Hugo Wolff died suddenly on the night of 27 to 28 August 2023, shortly after his company had celebrated 30 years of activity. Sibiu’s cultural community mourned him publicly.

“He was much more than a talented choreographer — he was a friend, a mentor and an inexhaustible source of inspiration.” University Cultural Center of Sibiu

In Hugo Wolff’s own words

“For me, dance means a continuous search.”

For me, dance means a continuous search. The everyday life of people everywhere, in all its aspects, sometimes banal, sometimes extraordinary, the roots, traditions and aspirations of these people — all of this stirs my imagination and gives me a feeling of constant restlessness. Through dance I try to preserve my freedom of spirit. Dance is an instinctive, extra-conscious necessity; it is a form of thinking, a function as vital as breathing.

The dance I conceive brings together every impulse, whether contradictory or complementary: love, anxiety, nostalgia, bewilderment, trust — but also mistrust, safety and insecurity, pride, disgust, weariness, humiliation. All of these urge me to conceive a new dance, to think of another means of expression.

With every dance I create, I rediscover myself. An imagined dance becomes “alive” and real, like a being that, once born, becomes bound to a field already explored. Once the dance takes shape, I realise that it was asking to be born, because it has its own destiny. I merely opened the gate for this “being” that had to be born, to grow and to live.

The roots of the dance I imagine are deeply embedded in the fundamental reality that is life, experienced in different ways. That is why I have given my company a certain flexibility in approaching different kinds of dance. In this way we have been able to include, within the broad register of modern and contemporary dance, creations of entirely different genres — from stylisations of Romanian ritual themes to dance with an oriental touch, dance theatre and even avant-garde symbolic dance. This does not mean that the company lacks a style of its own. On the contrary, our style is present in everything we do, but always in a different form.

Hugo Wolff — choreographer, founder of the Microbis Company

Parent institution

University Cultural Center of Sibiu

Microbis operates under the University Cultural Center of Sibiu, a public cultural institution founded at the same historical moment — practically the year the company was born. Alongside Microbis, the Center hosts other student artistic groups, from the “Ardealul” Folk Ensemble to street dance crews.

From the foreword to the Microbis Company album

There comes a moment when we feel the need to stop and look back, leafing through memories — photographs, posters, reviews, video recordings.

It seems like only yesterday… And yet 20 years have passed since a group of enthusiastic students from the Faculty of Engineering decided to spend their free time in an enjoyable and creative way by founding a student dance group.

It is true that most of us had done some dancing before, including ballet or gymnastics, which gave the group a certain discipline and a certain pace of work from the very beginning. The “launch on the student arts market” of the new “product”, MICROBIS, was aggressive, in the good sense of the word, and the diplomas and awards have never stopped coming.

This ever-young, energetic, enthusiastic world lives on, with other faces but the same ideals and hopes. Let us not forget, however, that life has a logic of ages that dictates preferences, rhythms and temperaments that are never identical. From choreographies belonging mainly to the modern style, the company has moved on to modern ballet and even to theatre dance.

Passion is the force that gives strength, and Microbis has had the strength needed to keep rising throughout all these years. Indeed, one of the secrets of the Microbis Company’s success has been, and still is, the cohesion and consistency of the team and its members — instilled, of course, by the company’s founder and choreographer, Hugo Wolff.

For all its ephemerality, the artistic act cannot exist without the hope of remaining in the memory of those who watch it. We invite you to turn the pages of the album slowly, pausing for a few seconds on each page, each story or photograph. Behind them lie feelings, and sometimes entire stories.

Dr. Eng. Liliana Georgeta Popescu — Director, University Cultural Center of Sibiu
