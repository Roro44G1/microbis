The founder’s story

From the Microbis Company album

The Power of Dance

You and I — in fact, all of us — when we hear a catchy tune, feel that nerve twitch inside us, pushing us either to tap out the rhythm or simply to throw ourselves onto the dance floor. With clumsy movements or, on the contrary, natural ones, more awkwardly or, conversely, more professionally, we have all danced at least once in our lives.

But what is DANCE? The DEX, the explanatory dictionary of the Romanian language, defines it as follows: “a set of rhythmic, varied movements of the human body performed to the rhythm of a melody, having the character of art or entertainment”.

Or we might put it this way: dance is a way of expressing feelings. For some of us — that is, for them — dance is life and life is dance.

I am speaking to you now — you who may be bored, or who may be curious and have started reading these lines! Do you have any idea how much work these young people put in, devoting their free time to dance, be it classical, modern or contemporary…? Do you have any idea what the struggle with passion means? It is a struggle in which you fight everyday hardships and the bureaucracy of a whole world, and in which art must, more often than not, prevail. The same happens to them. A troupe of students infected by this microbe went into quarantine and called themselves MICROBIS.

If I were to tell you more about them, you might lose some of the charm of discovering them yourself. Watch… and admire what they have created… now consider that everything you see is as young as a sandcastle… so you, at least, give them a round of applause.

Those who did not believe in them spurred them on; those who imposed rigour and constraints set them free — so don’t argue with the power of dance, and…

Dance! Dance! Dance!
